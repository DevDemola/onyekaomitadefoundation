Good to know

Who can get support from the foundation?

Families of children with autism and other developmental disabilities. Use the form or call us and we’ll guide you to the programme that fits.

Where do your programmes run?

Our base is in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and we run community programmes and outreach across Nigeria.

Does it cost anything to get support?

Contact us about your situation. We’ll explain what support is available for your family.

Can my organisation partner with you?

Yes. Schools, churches, companies and clinics partner with us on events, school placement and awareness.
