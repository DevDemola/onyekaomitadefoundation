About us

We exist because one girl was never understood.

The Onyeka Omitade Foundation supports children with autism and developmental disabilities across Nigeria, and the families and communities around them.

Our story

From one family’s pain to a promise for many.

Our founder grew up watching her older sister face exclusion and bullying because of developmental challenges that no one recognised or explained. There was no diagnosis, no support at school and very little kindness from the people around them.

That experience never left her. It became a question: how many other children in Nigeria are going through the same thing, unseen?

The Onyeka Omitade Foundation is her answer. We work so that children with autism and developmental differences are recognised early, supported properly and included fully, at school, at church and in their communities.

Mission

Inclusion, understanding and equal opportunity.

We promote inclusion, understanding and equal opportunities for children with special needs through education, awareness, therapy support and community-based programmes.

Vision

Every child included.

A Nigeria where every child, regardless of ability, is fully included, understood and supported to reach their highest potential.

What guides us

Our values

Dignity

Every child is a person first, never a diagnosis or a label.

Inclusion

Children of every ability belong in the same classrooms and communities.

Understanding

Knowledge replaces fear and stigma, so we teach and share it widely.

Family

When parents and caregivers are supported, children do better.

How we work

From first worry to full inclusion

The path we walk with each family. Our six programmes support every stage of it.

  1. Awareness

    Communities, schools and churches learn what autism is, so children are noticed and supported instead of judged.

  2. Assessment

    Families are guided to a professional assessment and an understanding of their child’s needs.

  3. School & therapy

    We help children get into school with support and connect families to therapy.

  4. Independence

    Life skills and confidence for the years ahead, with parents supported throughout.
