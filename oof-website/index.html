Beyond labels

A label is not a limit.

Children with autism are often called names before anyone tries to understand them. Through awareness in schools, churches and communities, we replace stigma with knowledge, so every child is met with patience instead of judgement.

  • “Stubborn”Overwhelmed, and learning to cope
  • “Not school material”Ready, with the right support
  • “A spiritual problem”A child who learns differently
  • “Hide them at home”Include them everywhere

Why we exist

It started with a sister nobody understood.

Our founder grew up watching her older sister face exclusion and bullying because of developmental challenges no one recognised or explained.

The Onyeka Omitade Foundation exists so that no other Nigerian family goes through that alone.
