FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Contact us

Who can get support from the foundation?

Families of children with autism and other developmental disabilities. Use the support form or call us, and we’ll guide you to the programme that fits.

Where do your programmes run?

Our base is in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and we run community programmes and outreach across Nigeria.

How are donations used?

Donations fund assessments, therapy support, school placement, awareness programmes and support for parents and caregivers.

How can I volunteer?

Fill in the volunteer form on our contact page. We welcome teachers, therapists, students, creatives and anyone willing to help at events.
