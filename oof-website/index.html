What we believe

In too many Nigerian homes, a child who learns differently is hidden, labelled or turned away from school. We believe that child belongs: in the classroom, in the church, at the party, and in the future.

Read our story

Founded in Port Harcourt, the Onyeka Omitade Foundation runs six programmes for children with autism and developmental disabilities, and for the families who love them.

Our story01 / 03
01 · The sister

It started with a girl nobody understood.

Our founder grew up watching her older sister face exclusion and bullying because of developmental challenges that no one recognised or explained.

02 · The question

How many more children are going through the same thing, unseen?

There was no diagnosis, no support at school and very little kindness. That experience became a question she couldn’t put down.

03 · The promise

So that no family walks this road alone.

The Onyeka Omitade Foundation is her answer. We help children get recognised early, supported properly and included fully, and we stand beside their families.
