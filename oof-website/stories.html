Delight’s journey

  1. BeforeAt home, no school
  2. SupportTherapy & assessment
  3. TodayMainstream school

Delight had never attended school. Like many children with developmental differences in Nigeria, she had been kept at home, with few people around her understanding what she needed.

Through the foundation, Delight received therapy support, and her family was helped to find a school ready to welcome her. Today she attends a mainstream school and learns alongside other children.

For parents

Early signs of autism to look out for.

Every child develops at their own pace, and no single sign means a child is autistic. Noticing differences early, and getting a professional assessment, can change a child’s future.

CommunicationNot responding to their name by about 12 months, or delayed speech
SocialLittle eye contact, or little interest in pointing, sharing or showing things
BehaviourRepeated movements like hand-flapping or rocking, or very strict routines
SensesStrong reactions to sounds, lights, textures or certain foods
PlayLining up toys, or focusing intensely on one part of an object
SkillsLosing words or skills they had before

This is a guide, not a diagnosis. If you’re worried, speak with a paediatrician or developmental specialist. We can help you work out where to start.
