Parent guide

Early signs of autism to look out for.

Every child develops at their own pace, and no single sign means a child is autistic. Noticing differences early, and getting a professional assessment, can change a child’s future.

Communication

Not responding to their name by about 12 months, or delayed speech

Social

Little eye contact, or little interest in pointing, sharing or showing things

Behaviour

Repeated movements like hand flapping or rocking, or very strict routines

Senses

Strong reactions to sounds, lights, textures or certain foods

Play

Lining up toys, or focusing intensely on one part of an object

Skills

Losing words or skills they had before

This is a guide, not a diagnosis. If you’re worried, speak with a paediatrician or developmental specialist. We can help you work out where to start.
