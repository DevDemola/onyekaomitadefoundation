Programmes

Six programmes.
One goal: no child left out.

Each programme meets a different need, from changing minds in the community to getting a child into school and preparing them for life.

Programme 01Communities & schools

Beyond Labels Initiative

Many children with autism are misunderstood long before they are supported. Beyond Labels takes clear, kind information about autism into schools, churches and communities, so teachers, neighbours and classmates respond with understanding instead of stigma.

  • Autism awareness sessions for schools and teachers
  • Community and faith-group outreach
  • Inclusive events like Play Without Label

Programme 02Children out of school

Back to School Without Barriers

Too many children with developmental differences are kept at home or turned away from school. We help families get a professional assessment, find a school that will welcome their child, and put the right classroom support in place.

  • Professional developmental assessments
  • School placement and enrolment support
  • Ongoing classroom and learning support

Programme 03Parents & caregivers

Onyeka Family Circle

Raising a child with autism can feel lonely. The Family Circle connects parents and caregivers with others who understand, along with practical guidance on therapy, schooling and everyday life at home.

  • Parent and caregiver support meetings
  • Guidance on therapy and next steps
  • Shared resources and encouragement

Programme 04Older children & teens

FutureAble

As children grow, the goal is a life with as much independence and dignity as possible. FutureAble builds practical life skills and confidence for children and young people with developmental disabilities.

  • Everyday life-skills training
  • Confidence and social-skills building
  • Preparing for independence

Programme 05Every child

Every Child Thrives

Every child deserves joy. Every Child Thrives focuses on growth and well-being through play, creativity and inclusive activities where children of all abilities spend time together.

  • Creative and play-based activities
  • Well-being and growth support
  • Inclusive group activities
Fund this programmeRefer a child
Programme 06Mothers & caregivers

Women Rising Initiative

Mothers and female caregivers often carry the heaviest load. Women Rising offers empowerment and maternal support, so the women caring for children with special needs are supported too.

  • Caregiver empowerment programmes
  • Maternal and emotional support
  • Community among women caregivers
