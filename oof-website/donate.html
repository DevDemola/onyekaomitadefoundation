Questions

Giving FAQs

How are donations used?

Donations fund assessments, therapy support, school placement, awareness programmes and support for parents and caregivers.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Choose “Give monthly” above. Monthly gifts help us plan support families can rely on.

Is my payment secure?

Payments are processed by a secure payment provider. We never see or store your card details.

Can I give from outside Nigeria?

Yes. International cards are accepted through our payment page, and you can also give through our crowdfunding page.
